Perth Transit Boards

Platform-style departure signs for Perth trains and buses, built from the free public transport timetable for Western Australia. Pick a station or type a bus stop number and the sign counts down on your device's own clock.

Platform 2
Train board
Any train station. Two platform signs, all lines merged, next three services, scrolling stop list, flashing when a train is due.
Stop 12808
Bus stop board
Type the number from the green bus stop sign. Every route at that stop, next six departures, e-paper style.

The boards load the timetable automatically if google_transit.zip sits next to this page. Otherwise, download the timetable and tap Import on either board. Times are scheduled, not live.
Timetable data is available free of charge from www.transperth.wa.gov.au. This is an independent project and is not endorsed by the data provider.
