Perth Transit Boards

Free train platform signs and bus stop boards for Perth, running right in your browser. Pick a station, type a stop number, and watch the next service count down on your own phone, tablet or spare screen.

Who it's for

No app, no account, no ads. If any of these sound like you, you're in the right place.

Rail fansA platform sign for any station on the network, flashing when the train is due, just like the real thing.
Bus spottersEvery route at your stop in one list, in the style of the new city e-paper signs.
TinkerersTurn an old phone or tablet into a desk display. Each board is one HTML file: read it, change it, make it yours.

What's inside

Train board

  • Any station, picked from a list grouped by line (Armadale, Thornlie-Cockburn, Mandurah, Yanchep and more) or A to Z.
  • Two signs side by side, one per platform, with every line through the station merged in time order.
  • The next train large, the following two under "Then:".
  • A scrolling "Stops at" line listing every station to the end of the line.
  • Minutes round up like the real boards. At 1 minute the sign flashes, and the train stays up until 25 seconds after its scheduled time.
  • Demo mode, full screen, and text that sizes itself to fill the screen.

Bus stop board

  • Type the stop number shown at the bus stop, for example 12808.
  • The next six departures from every route at that stop: route, destination and minutes.
  • "Now", "5 mins", or a clock time for buses more than 30 minutes away.
  • Full screen and auto-sizing text, so it reads well on any screen.

Try it in 30 seconds

  1. Open the train board or the bus stop board.
  2. The timetable loads by itself. The first visit takes a moment while it reads the file (about 26 MB), then it's saved on your device.
  3. Train board: tap Pick station. Bus board: type a stop number and tap Show stop.
  4. Tap Full screen, turn your phone sideways, and leave it running.

If the timetable doesn't load (for example when a board is opened as a downloaded file), tap Download timetable, then Import and choose the file.

Put it on a spare screen

Got an old phone in a drawer? Prop it up by the door, open a board, tap Full screen and it becomes a departure sign. The screen stays awake while it runs, and the countdown follows the device's own clock, accurate to the second.

In Chrome on Android, the menu has Add to Home screen, so a board opens like an app.

Make it yours

Each board is a single HTML file. A few numbers near the top of its script change how it behaves:

BoardSettingDefaultWhat it does
TrainSTAY_SECONDS25Seconds a train stays (flashing 1) after its scheduled time
TrainFLASH_MS650Length of each flash step
TrainSCROLL_MS800Speed of the scrolling stop list (higher is slower)
TrainTIMER_OFFSET_SECONDS0Shifts every countdown; negative gives a leave-early buffer
BusMINUTES_UP_TO30Show minutes up to this, then clock times
BusROWS6Number of departures shown

Want to build on it? The source is on GitHub. Fork it, change it, share what you make.

How it works

Everything happens on your device. The page opens the official public timetable (a GTFS file), reads its 75 MB of stop times line by line, and keeps only what it needs: train trips for the train board, one stop's departures for the bus board. Service days, public holidays and published closures come from the timetable's own calendar. There's no server and no tracking, and nothing is sent anywhere.

The fine print

Times are scheduled, not live. There's no public real-time feed, so delays and replacement-bus notices aren't shown. Check official sources before you travel.
